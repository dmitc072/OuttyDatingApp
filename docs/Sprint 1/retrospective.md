**Prepared by:** Duane Mitchell (Scrum Master)

**Team Participation:** This retrospective was prepared by the Scrum Master because a team retrospective meeting could not be held. Attempts were made to coordinate with team members during Sprint 1, but a meeting could not be scheduled due to limited participation and availability.

---

## Format Used: Start / Stop / Continue

---

## What Went Well (Continue)

- **Project foundation was established** — the team successfully created the GitHub repository, GitHub Projects board, and initial project structure for future development.
- **Sprint artifacts were completed** — the team produced the required Scrum documentation, including the sprint backlog, burndown chart, daily scrums, sprint review, and retrospective.

- **The project has a foundation for Sprint 2** — despite limited progress, the repository, backlog, and project structure are ready for continued development.

---

## What Didn't Go Well (Stop)

- **Environment setup ate Day 1 and Day 2** — we underestimated how long installing MAUI workloads, and Azure CLI would take. Should have done this in a Week 0 before the sprint started
- **GitHub Projects was not updated consistently** — task statuses were not always kept current, making it harder to track sprint progress.
- **Team participation was inconsistent** — one team member did not contribute to the Outty project during Sprint 1, which increased the workload for the remaining team members and slowed overall progress.

- **A Sprint Retrospective meeting could not be held** — the team was unable to coordinate a meeting before the end of Sprint 1.

---

## What To Do Differently (Start)

- **Sprint 2: Run a Week 0 environment check** — confirm all tools installed and Azure connections working before Sprint 2 Day 1
- **Sprint 2: Update GitHub Projects daily as part of the daily scrum** — review and update task status during each scrum.
- **Sprint 2: Schedule two planned pairing sessions per week** — put them on the calendar at sprint planning, not reactively
- **Sprint 2: Confirm team availability during Sprint Planning** — verify that each team member is able to participate and reassign work early if someone becomes unavailable.

- **Sprint 2: Establish a team communication expectation** — respond to team messages within 24 hours whenever possible so issues can be identified and addressed earlier.
